About Us

Wanderly is a small team of travel lovers on a mission to make discovering your next destination effortless. We curate beaches, temples, and cities from around the globe and match them to whatever you're in the mood to explore — whether that's sun and sand, ancient history, or a buzzing new city.

Guidance for Team Members

  • Keep destination descriptions short, accurate, and welcoming — one to two sentences per place.
  • New destinations go into travel_recommendation_api.json under the correct category (countries, temples, or beaches).
  • Always test the Search and Clear buttons after adding or editing data.
  • Use royalty-free, high-resolution images for every new listing.
  • Report bugs or content issues through the Contact Us section below.
